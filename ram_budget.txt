# The budget of internal RAM for this app, read by the SDK's tools/check_ram.sh
# (catalog rule B8):

#   <kode-sdk>/tools/check_ram.sh build/my_app.elf ram_budget.txt
#
# It starts from the SDK's tests/target/ram_budget.txt at the pinned release,
# without the lines of what this app turns off (the radio, the network, BLE and
# LVGL), and adds the app's own large statics at the end, each with its reason.
